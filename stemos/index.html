 About stemOS

What is stemOS?

stemOS is a LATAM + US native platform that combines a skills graph engine, a Socratic AI tutor (Feynman Engine), and Open Badges 3.0 credentials — all running on local GenAI infrastructure. Our mission: to make high-quality technical English and STEM education accessible to the next generation of engineers, technicians, and innovators.
