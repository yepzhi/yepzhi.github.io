## 🌟 Overview

**RichmondAI** is a purpose-built instructional material generator tailored for English Language Teachers (ELT) and English for Specific Purposes (ESP) instructors across engineering, medical, tech, business, and aviation tracks.

Unlike generic LLMs, RichmondAI calibrates every generation directly to the **Common European Framework of Reference (CEFR: A1 to C2)**, accommodates **neurodiverse learning needs (SEND)** like Dyslexia and ADHD, and offers an interactive **print-ready cut-out flashcard generator**, student quiz worksheets, and DOCX exports.

## 🚀 Generator Suite

1. **Multiple-Choice Questions (MCQ)** — Leveled quizzes with answer keys & pedagogical rationale.
2. **Cut-out Flash Cards** — Printable 2-sided vocabulary cards with IPA phonetics and CEFR tags.
3. **Interactive Learning Activities** — Communicative classroom games, warmers, and roleplays.
4. **YouTube Video Content Generator** — Quizzes and transcripts pulled from YouTube links.
5. **Feedback & Rubric Generator** — Detailed formative/summative feedback on student essays.
6. **Text Summariser & CEFR Leveler** — Simplify or elevate any reading passage from A1 up to C2.
7. **Inspiration & Hook Generator** — High-engagement lesson hooks for reluctant learners.
8. **Classroom Questions** — Bloom's Taxonomy leveled inquiry prompts.
9. **Very Short Answers** — Fast-paced 1-word or short phrase retrieval quizzes.
10. **Discussion Topics & Debate Motions** — Engaging speaking prompts with target functional exponents.
11. **ESP Engineering & Tech** — Technical English for shop floors, schematics, and coding.
12. **ESP Healthcare & Nursing** — Triage roleplays, bedside empathy, and medical documentation.
13. **ESP Business & Aviation** — Formal negotiations, executive memos, and ICAO phraseology.

## 🎨 Design & UI Architecture

RichmondAI is inspired by `stemOS` with a modern cyber-aurora aesthetic:
- **Colors**: Pink (`#ec4899`, `#f43f5e`), Electric Blue (`#38bdf8`, `#6366f1`), Gold (`#f59e0b`, `#fbbf24`), on deep obsidian (`#060d24`).
- **Aurora Canvas**: Dynamic interactive canvas with floating orbs and reactive particle dots.
- **Glassmorphic Navigation**: Collapsible rail sidebar + floating top island header.
- **Client-Side Privacy**: Personal Gemini API key stored securely in local browser storage.
