Our Cut-out Flashcards Generator creates 2-sided vocabulary cards with target terms, IPA phonetics, CEFR band tags, clear technical definitions, and contextual example sentences.

On screen, cards flip in 3D when clicked. When you click Print / PDF, our print stylesheet automatically formats the cards into a perforated grid with dashed cutting guidelines (✂ Cut along dashed line), ready for quick scissor cutting in the classroom.

RichmondAI embeds multi-modal inclusive accommodations directly into the prompt pipeline:

  • Dyslexia: High visual clarity, bulleted micro-chunking, avoidance of dense walls of text.
  • ADHD: Short gamified tasks, bold keyword anchors, interactive check-ins.
  • Autism Spectrum (ASD): Explicit, step-by-step instructions, literal phrasing that avoids confusing idioms or sarcasm.
  • Speech & Language: Visual phonics cues, paired oral-written prompts.
