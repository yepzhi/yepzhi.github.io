# JobHunter — The Remote Job System

> **"Trabajar remoto no es suerte, es estrategia."**  
> An elite Remote Career Strategist, Technical Recruiter, and Headhunter AI platform replicating the complete 4-module framework of **The Remote Job System** by [@reginaontheclock](https://instagram.com/reginaontheclock).

Live deployment: [yepzhi.com/jobhunter](https://yepzhi.com/jobhunter)

---

## 🎯 Target Audience & Core Mission
Tailored for ambitious professionals in Latin America and international markets seeking full-time remote roles with US and global tech startups, paid in USD via direct contractor agreements (**W-8BEN**) or Employer of Record (**Deel / Remote.com**).

---

## ⚡ The 4 Modules & 90-Day Execution Engine

### 1. ATS-Compliant CV Engine (`/cv_tailor`)
- **Strict 1-Column Compliance**: Zero graphic elements, tables, icons, or photos that cause ATS scanners to reject applications.
- **Google X-Y-Z Achievement Formula**: Automatically rephrases experience bullets into *"Accomplished [X], as measured by [Y], by doing [Z]"*.
- **Job Description Keyword Parser**: Identifies top 10 hard skills, software tools, and underlying company pain points with real-time match scoring (0–100%).
- **Export Options**: 1-click clipboard copy, plain text (`.txt`) download, or print-ready PDF view.

### 2. LinkedIn Magnet Optimization (`/linkedin`)
- **High-Impact Headline Formula** (Max 220 chars): `[Role] | [Specialization] | Helping [Companies] achieve [Outcome] | [Top 3 Tools] | Bilingual`.
- **4-Part About Section**: Hook, core strengths & track record, remote/async work philosophy (Notion, Slack, Loom), and direct CTA.
- **US Market Re-titling**: Converts local job titles to standardized US market terminology.
- **Recruiter Search Checklist**: Open to Work targeting US/Worldwide remote, 50 skills tags optimization.

### 3. Sourcing & Hiring Manager Direct Outreach (`/search` & `/outreach`)
- **Boolean Search Query Generator**: Ready-to-copy strings for LinkedIn Jobs and direct Google X-Ray searches into **Greenhouse**, **Lever**, and **Ashby** career boards.
- **Curated Remote Platforms Directory**: Quick access to Wellfound (AngelList), RemoteOK, We Work Remotely, Himalayas, Remotive, and Torre.
- **3-Step Outreach Sequence**:
  - *Template A*: Connection Request (< 300 chars).
  - *Template B*: Value Pitch InMail / Post-Connection Message.
  - *Template C*: 7-Day Follow-Up Message.
- **Remote Scam Detection Scanner**: Identifies red flags (Telegram-only interviews, equipment reimbursement checks, fake public domains).

### 4. Interview Mastery & Salary Negotiation (`/interview` & `/negotiate`)
- **STAR Method Mock Interview Simulator**: Practice elevator pitches ("Tell Me About Yourself" in 90 seconds), cross-timezone collaboration, and handling ambiguity with voice TTS playback.
- **Real-Time Evaluator**: Scores answers (0–100) based on quantifiable metrics and generates high-impact model answers.
- **Strategic Reverse Questions**: Proven questions to ask the hiring team to demonstrate senior strategic thinking.
- **Salary Counter-Offer Calculator**: Exact scripts to deflect salary questions during initial screening, formulate a **+10–15% counter-offer**, and negotiate equipment stipends ($500–$1,000 USD).

## 📅 The 90-Day Tactical Calendar (`/daily_task`)
- **Phase 1: Foundation & Setup** (Weeks 1–2): Intake calibration, Google X-Y-Z CV overhaul, LinkedIn rewrite, top 30 target company list.
- **Phase 2: Sourcing & Outreach** (Weeks 3–6): 30 min daily Boolean search, 1–2 tailored applications, direct hiring manager outreach (no generic "spray and pray").
- **Phase 3: Pipeline & Prep** (Weeks 7–9): 5–7 day follow-ups, daily voice STAR drills, elevator pitch mastery.
- **Phase 4: Final Rounds & Closing** (Weeks 10–12): Executive rounds, counter-offer negotiations, W-8BEN contractor tax review.

## 🛠️ Tech Stack & Architecture
- **Vanilla Modern JavaScript & HTML5**: Zero external heavyweight frameworks for sub-millisecond load times.
- **Tailored Modern CSS**: Sleek dark terminal meets executive recruiter aesthetic, glassmorphic cards, touch-friendly 44px buttons, mobile-first responsive layout with bottom dock navigation.
- **Web Speech API**: Integrated Text-To-Speech (TTS) for voice mock interview simulations.
- **Local Storage Engine**: Automatic offline persistence for candidate profiles, completed tasks, and generated artifacts.
